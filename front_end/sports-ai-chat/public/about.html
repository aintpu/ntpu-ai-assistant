系統特色Features

為什麼好用Why it helps

不只是搜尋，而是「先找到正確資料、再由 AI 整理成通順回答」，兼顧速度、正確與可查證。More than search: it retrieves the right material first, then has AI compose a clear answer — fast, accurate, and verifiable.

口語就能問Ask in plain words

用日常語言提問即可，不必記住繁瑣的辦法名稱，也不用在多個網站間切換。Just type a question the way you'd say it — no need to recall exact regulation titles or hop between sites.

附官方來源Official sources cited

每則回答都附上資料來源連結，點擊即可前往官方原文查證，資訊可信不猜測。Every answer includes a link to the official page, so you can verify the original text yourself.

全天候可用Available 24/7

24 小時隨時查詢，免安裝、免登入，減少臨櫃與電話往返的等待。Query any time, day or night — no install, no login, no waiting on the phone or at a counter.

雙搜尋更準Hybrid retrieval

同時用「語意搜尋」與「關鍵字搜尋」找資料，兼顧理解語意與精準比對。Combines semantic and keyword search to match both meaning and exact terms.

中英雙語Bilingual

介面與回答支援中英文，服務國際學生與外籍教師。Interface and answers in both Chinese and English for international students and faculty.

可持續擴充Built to grow

模組化設計，未來可逐步納入更多處室，服務範圍持續擴大。A modular design lets more offices join over time, expanding coverage step by step.

系統架構Architecture

一個問題，如何變成答案From question to answer

系統採「先檢索、再生成」（RAG）的作法：先從知識庫找出最相關的官方資料，再交由 AI 整理成通順回答。The system uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): it first finds the most relevant official material, then has AI compose a readable answer.

Step 1

判斷處室Route

辨識問題屬於哪一個處室的範圍。Identify which office the question belongs to.

Step 2

檢索資料Retrieve

語意 + 關鍵字雙搜尋，找出最相關內容。Semantic + keyword search find the best matches.

Step 3

AI 整理Generate

語言模型將資料整理成通順回答。The language model composes a clear answer.

Step 4

附上來源Cite

回傳答案並附官方資料連結。Return the answer with official source links.

知識庫由各處室公開資料經爬蟲蒐集、整理與人工校正而成。The knowledge base is built from public office data via crawling, curation, and manual correction.

前端 · 使用者介面Frontend · Interface

Next.js · React · Tailwind CSS

你在瀏覽器操作的問答網頁，支援中英雙語與快速問題。The Q&A web page you interact with — bilingual, with quick questions.

後端 · 核心服務Backend · Core service

Python · FastAPI · FAISS · BM25

負責檢索知識庫、呼叫 AI 產生回答，並提供語音輸入。Retrieves from the knowledge base, calls the AI, and supports voice input.

AI 模型AI models

GPT-5.4-mini · GPT-4o-mini · Whisper

主模型負責理解問題與產生回答；輔助模型處理處室判斷、問題改寫與結果排序；Whisper 支援語音辨識。The main model interprets questions and composes answers; a lighter model handles routing, query rewriting, and re-ranking; Whisper powers speech recognition.

部署 · 雲端平台Deployment · Cloud

GCP Cloud Run · Secret Manager

部署於 Google 雲端，前後端各為獨立服務，金鑰安全託管。Runs on Google Cloud; frontend and backend are separate services with managed secrets.

使用須知Notice

使用前，請留意這些Before you rely on it

重要提醒Important reminders

  • 1回答由 AI 自動生成，僅供參考，可能有不完整或過時之處。Answers are AI-generated and for reference only; they may be incomplete or out of date.
  • 2涉及個人權益的事項（學分認定、免修資格、報名期限等），請以各處室官方公告或正式函文為準，並向承辦單位確認。For matters affecting your rights (credit recognition, exemption eligibility, deadlines), rely on official announcements and confirm with the responsible office.
  • 3建議點擊回答附上的資料來源連結，查看官方原文。Please click the source links in each answer to read the official text.
  • 4請勿在對話中輸入個人敏感資料（身分證字號、密碼、金融資訊等）。Do not enter sensitive personal data (ID numbers, passwords, financial info) in the chat.
  • 5知識庫為定期整理，非即時同步；最新或緊急公告仍以處室官網為準。The knowledge base is updated periodically, not in real time; for the latest or urgent notices, check the office's official site.
